## Physical properties

<img src="/tsost/course/media/sound.svg" alt="Sound">

- **Frequency** — the number of cycles per second (Hz). We perceive frequency as *pitch*.
- **Amplitude** — the magnitude of a wave's displacement. We perceive amplitude as *loudness*.
- **Timbre** — the spectral complexity of a sound. We perceive timbre as *color* — what makes a violin sound different from a flute at the same pitch and loudness.
- **Duration** — the temporal interval of a sound: its envelope (onset, sustain, decay).
