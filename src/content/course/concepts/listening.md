> *"Hearing is a physiological phenomenon; listening is a psychological act."*
> — Roland Barthes, *Listening*, 1985

## Modes of listening

Three modes, drawn from Pierre Schaeffer[^1] and Michel Chion[^2]:

1. **Causal listening** — listening to identify the source of a sound. *What made that noise?*

2. **Semantic listening** — listening for meaning, as in speech or language. *What is being said?*

3. **Reduced listening** — listening to the sound itself, separated from its source or meaning. *What does this sound like, as pure sonic material?*
