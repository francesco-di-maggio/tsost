## Symbolic vs Expressive

**Symbolic sound** — sounds that carry agreed meaning: alarms, notifications, status tones. The meaning is learned. A beep means *something happened*.

**Expressive sound** — sounds that communicate through their own qualities: texture, rhythm, dynamics. The meaning is perceived. A sound that increases in pitch as you approach something communicates proximity through its own behavior.

## Producing

Sound can be recorded and played back, or generated from scratch.

**Sampling** — playback of recorded sound. A sample can be triggered, looped, read from different positions, and altered in speed and pitch.

**Additive synthesis** — a tone built by summing sine waves, each partial with its own frequency and amplitude.

**Subtractive synthesis** — a rich source waveform shaped by removing frequencies with filters.

## Processing

Shaping sound after it is produced.

**Filtering** — boosting or removing frequency ranges to change tone.

**Reverb and delay** — adding reflections and echoes to place a sound in a space.

**Distortion** — adding harmonics by overdriving the signal.

**Modulation** — varying a parameter over time with an oscillator, as in tremolo, vibrato, or chorus.
