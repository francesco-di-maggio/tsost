Psychoacoustics is the study of how humans perceive sound — the relationship between the physical properties of a sound wave and the subjective experience of hearing it. 

## Key phenomena

**Loudness** — perceived volume does not scale linearly with amplitude. Small changes at low volumes are far more noticeable than the same changes at high volumes.

**Pitch** — perceived frequency is non-linear. A musical octave doubles frequency, but we hear each octave as an equal step.

**Timbre** — the quality that distinguishes two sounds at the same pitch and loudness. It is why a violin and a flute sound different.
