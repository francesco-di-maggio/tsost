Thing-centeredness is a design perspective that takes the object as the starting point for design, rather than the user or the system.

Instead of asking "what does the user need?", a thing-centered approach asks: *what is the character of this thing? what are its properties, behaviors, affordances? what would be a natural extension of what it already is?*

## Methods

**Object diary** — spend a day observing your object. When is it used? By whom? How does it change throughout the day?

**Character analysis** — what are the essential qualities of this object? What makes it recognizably itself?

**Inter-thing correspondence** — how does your object relate to other objects around it? What is its social life?

**Thing-personification** — if your object were a person, who would it be? What would it say? What would it notice?
