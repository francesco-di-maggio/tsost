## Homework — Pairs

Refine what you started in the workshop.

1. Define your [mapping](/tsost/course/concepts/mapping/) — what sensor values trigger what sound parameters, and why?
2. Iterate on your patch in [Max](/tsost/course/tools/max/) or [Plugdata](/tsost/course/tools/plugdata/) — test it with your object
3. Does the sound behavior fit the character of the thing?
4. Document your rule set and design decisions — you will need these for [Assignment 3](/tsost/course/assignments/assignment-3/)
