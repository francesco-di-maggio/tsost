## Brief

Make an audiocumentary (audio documentary) of a typical day in your life that gives insight into your everyday soundscape. 

Not the extraordinary — the ordinary. The coffee machine, the tram, the rain on glass, the supermarket, the squeak of the door, the sound of your own footsteps.

## Requirements

- Maximum length: 5 minutes
- Any recorder — phone, handy recorder

## Process

**Recording**

Get close to sources. Avoid wind — unless wind is what you are documenting. Record more than you think you need; you will cut later. Capture at least one minute of ambient sound per location.

**Editing**

Import your clips into **[Audacity](https://www.audacityteam.org)** (free, Mac and Windows), **GarageBand** (free, Mac, pre-installed), or any other editor you are familiar with. Cut what doesn't serve the piece. Use fades at the start and end of each clip to avoid hard cuts. 

## Deliverable

Export as MP3 and upload to Canvas. In [Week 2](/tsost/course/weeks/week-2/) you will listen to each other's work in pairs and build a shared sound vocabulary.
