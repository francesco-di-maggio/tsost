## Brief

Document the design of your Smart Thing and reflect on your learning during the course.

## Report

The report supports and substantiates the design of your Smart Thing. It is a design rationale, not a technical manual. Maximum 4 pages.

It should cover four things:

**1. The object**

Which object did you choose and why? Ground your choice in the [thing-centered](/tsost/course/concepts/thing-centeredness/) methods you used: what did the object diary, character analysis, and other exercises reveal? What made this object a good candidate for a smart, sound-driven behavior?

**2. The sensor**

Which sensor did you choose and why? How does it connect to the character of the object and to the interaction you wanted to enable?

**3. The mapping and sound behavior**

What are the rules? Describe your mapping in plain language: what sensor values trigger what, and how does the sound respond. Why did you make these choices? Include any non-linear scaling, thresholds, or conditions that shape the behavior.

**4. The sound design**

What does your object sound like, and why? What guided your sound choices — synthesis method, timbre, register, rhythm? How does the sound relate to the object's character?

## Reflection

A personal reflection on your learning during the course. Maximum 1 page.

It should:

- Identify two or three concrete things you learned — about sound, about design, or about working with sensors, mapping, and code
- Reference specific moments from the homework and in-class exercises where relevant
- Reflect on your object's behavior in the collective installation at the Lucid.Bar: what did it sound like alone, and what happened when everything played together?
