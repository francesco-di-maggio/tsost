## Brief

Design and build a Smart Thing — a physical object from the Lucid.Bar, made smart through a sensor and a sound behavior that corresponds to the character of that object.

Your Smart Thing will be presented at the [Furniture Music at the Lucid.Bar](/tsost/course/weeks/week-8/).

## Process

**Week 4 — Choose your object and sensor**

- Pick an object from the Lucid.Bar (must have dynamic properties — see [Lucid.Bar Whitelist](/tsost/course/resources/lucid-bar-whitelist/))
- Conduct an object-centered study: one-day object diary, character analysis, inter-thing correspondence, thing-personification
- Choose a sensor (see [Lucid.Bar Whitelist](/tsost/course/resources/lucid-bar-whitelist/)) that connects to how you see the object being smart

**Week 5 — Pitch + ethnography**

- Present your object, sensor choice, and object-centered ethnographic study to the group
- Receive peer-to-peer feedback

**Week 6 — Mapping and sound design**

- Define your mapping (sensor values → sound parameters)
- Build a first patch in Max or Plugdata that responds to live data

**Week 7 — Intermediate feedback**

- Present work in progress, receive feedback

**Week 8 — Event**

- Your working Smart Thing is installed and running at the Lucid.Bar

## Deliverable

Submit a short video documentation of your working Smart Thing to Canvas by the deadline — show the object, the sensor in use, and the sound behavior it produces. Include your Max or Plugdata patch and your Arduino sketch.
