A Max package that connects patches, phones and devices over MQTT. Devices follow the Homie 5 convention, so Max can discover them and their values.

The Remote runs in any browser. It publishes touch, motion and mic level from a phone, tablet or computer as Homie values. The node on this site uses the same convention.
