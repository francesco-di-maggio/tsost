## Learning goals

- Develop a shared vocabulary for listening to and describing sound
- Create an audiocumentary of your everyday soundscape
- Study a physical object and design a sound-driven behaviour for it
- Build a working wireless sensor-to-sound prototype
- Contribute a smart object to a distributed sound installation and observe what emerges

The course closed with *Furniture Music at the Lucid.Bar* (16 June 2026): each pair's object ran independently in one space, without a central system.
