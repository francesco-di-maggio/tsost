Playfulness is a recurring yet undertheorised dimension of digital musical instrument design and human–AI music interaction. Although NIME practitioners have long engaged with playful interaction, the experiential and structural conditions that make musical interaction feel playful have rarely been treated as a primary object of inquiry. This workshop brings together practitioners, designers, and researchers to examine playful AI music systems through hands-on interaction and collaborative analysis.
